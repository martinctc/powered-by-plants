# My personal Veganuary Awards

**February 2, 2026**

After another successful Veganuary in 2026 - this time my motivation bolstered by a number of committed friends joining - I thought that it would be good for me to actually spend some time writing up some of my favourite restaurants, recipes, and tips from this month. I won't pretend to be a foodie influencer who's scoured the entirety of London for the best vegan dishes and restaurants - with the dismal January weather this year and seasonal affective disorder kicking in, I wouldn't say I've been particularly adventurous this time, and I've mostly settled on known comforts. 

Anyway, here are my top picks:

## Top Restaurants

1. Dauns
2. Unity Diner
3. Tofu Vegan

Both Dauns and Unity Diner were plant-based restaurants that we've newly discovered this month, and both happen to be on the same street (Wentworth Street) not far from Aldgate East. What are the odds? It's basically become our little vegan pilgrimage route.

**Dauns** is a Scandinavian café / diner that is relaxing, welcoming, and has 'Analog Journal' vibes - would definitely recommend their Smørrebrød and Swedish meatballs. You can also buy their selection of vegan cheese and hotdogs - which I'm typically not a fan of - but are actually really good! 

**Unity Diner** has to be recommended also because of their plant-based doner. As someone who used to (embarrassingly) survive on kebabs during my university days, I would say that their doner is actually really on point. They've nailed the post-night-out comfort food vibe, except you can eat it at a reasonable hour without regret.

Finally, if you haven't tried **Tofu Vegan**, then you should. It definitely ranks top as my favourite vegan restaurant in London, but this time I've put Dauns and Unity Diner ahead purely as they're the two new favourites I've tried this Veganuary. Tofu Vegan does incredible Chinese dishes and their menu is big - it's the kind of place where you need to go multiple times just to scratch the surface.

## Top Tips

Here's just a number of tips that came to mind this Veganuary, mostly learned through trial, error, and pleasant surprises.

**1. Just ask for it!**
When you're out dining at a non-plant-based restaurant and the food doesn't look very vegan, don't be afraid to just ask what they can do. There have been multiple occasions where we managed to discover a whole new 'hidden menu' that's vegan-friendly. 

**2. Lotus root is great in a hotpot**
I don't know why this was a revelation to me this year, but lotus root in hotpot is a game-changer. It stays crunchy even after cooking, has this subtle sweetness, and looks beautiful on the table. Plus it's fun to eat - who doesn't love food with holes in it?

**3. If you've bought too much veg, blend it and make a smoothie**
That slightly sad spinach? The carrots you forgot about? Chuck them in a blender with some oat milk, apples, plant protein, and a banana, and suddenly you've got breakfast sorted. It's not always pretty, but it works. 

**4. Try TVP (textured vegetable protein)**
It's cheap, convenient, and nutritious. I was skeptical at first because it looks like cat kibble, but once you rehydrate it and season it properly, it's genuinely good in noodles, as a stir fry dish, or even hotpot. 

**5. Try a 'no waste' subscription box**
It will encourage you to cook more and get creative with ingredients you might not normally buy. We used Oddbox this year and we cooked so much more than usual - partially because we felt guilty wasting the veg, but also because it forced us to try new things. Some weeks were more successful than others, but overall it massively expanded our repertoire.

## Final notes

Finally, one observation from this Veganuary amongst our group is that there definitely wasn't enough time to try everything we wanted to within the month of Veganuary. This just goes to show how many options you get even if you lead a plant-based diet, at least in London. After this month, we're definitely going to be expanding the proportion of our plant-based meals this year.
